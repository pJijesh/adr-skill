## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-11

### Added

- The adr skill: when to write a decision record, how to interview for it, and how to write it with honest consequences.
- A zero-dependency CLI: new, supersede, status, lint (20 rules) and index.
- A worked decision log for a fictional bookshop, linted clean.
- Installers for Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and Cursor, with generated adapters.
- Community files, citation metadata and a Dependabot configuration.
